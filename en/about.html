About Me

I am Diego Cerezo Rojas, a recently graduated Computer Engineering student with a strong interest in computer architecture, digital design, and low-level systems.

My primary career goal is to work as a hardware engineer designing processors, digital systems, and FPGA-based solutions. I am particularly interested in computer architecture, VHDL, processor design, microarchitecture, instruction set architectures, embedded systems, and hardware/software co-design.

In addition to hardware, I enjoy low-level software development, including assembly language, emulators, assemblers, compilers, and operating systems.
